About This Property

An Investment That Lives With You

Golden Grove is a thoughtfully planned 55-acre plantation community in Malavalli, near Shivanamudra Falls, Mandya, designed for those looking to own a peaceful nature retreat while enjoying the benefits of a professionally developed plantation community.

With 319 farm plots surrounded by a fully developed 8-10 year old Burma Teak plantation, Golden Grove brings together nature-inspired living, plantation ownership and lifestyle amenities in one destination.

The community features 20-foot internal roads, individual drip irrigation, 24x7 security and CCTV surveillance, along with a range of lifestyle amenities including a premium clubhouse, swimming pool, luxury guest cottages, multi-sports arena, children's play area, open party lawn and amphitheatre.

The property also includes natural and eco-focused features such as a scenic lake, man-made pond, landscaped green spaces and a dedicated Gaushala, creating a peaceful getaway from the city.

Located near Shivanasamudra Falls, Golden Grove offers access to popular attractions including Gaganachukki Falls, Barachukki Falls, Mutthathi, Bheemeshwari and Somanathapura Temple.
