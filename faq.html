Managed farmland is an agricultural property where professional teams handle day-to-day operations, maintenance, and security on behalf of the owner. The owner holds the legal title, while the management company ensures the land remains productive, secure, and well-maintained.

Regular agricultural land requires the owner to actively manage farming activities, labor, and upkeep. Managed farmland comes with a service agreement where a professional team handles these responsibilities, making it ideal for investors or weekend landowners who cannot commit full-time.

A dedicated on-site maintenance team, often provided by the developer or a specialized farm management company, maintains the property. This includes caretakers, horticulturists, and security personnel.

Services generally include watering, pruning, soil enrichment, pest control, security (CCTV and guards), road upkeep, and general landscaping. Some premium projects also offer harvesting services and guest cottage upkeep.
