Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering, scheduling land audits, or submitting enquiries. This includes:

  • Personal details: Name, email address, phone number, and physical billing addresses.
  • Professional profiles: Company registrations and developer license numbers for Seller Agents.
  • Property attachments: Land zoning maps, survey boundary logs, soil check charts, and yield histories uploaded by sellers.

2. How We Use Your Data

Farmland Bazaar v2 processes your details to establish a secure, verified agricultural assets marketplace. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Match prospective buyers with verified farmland listings and developers.
  • Validate the authenticity of boundary reports and soil qualities.
  • Send automatic updates about site visit schedules, negotiations, and replies.
  • Protect the integrity of escrows and prevent fraudulent registrations.

3. Data Security Measures

We deploy robust security safeguards to protect your personal details and land boundary attachments from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure:

  • Encrypted storage: Sensitive documents and files are stored on secure cloud repositories using advanced AES-256 protocols.
  • Secure transmissions: All website interactions and session logs use HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • IP Verification: Account settings logs monitor login actions to alert you of suspicious logins.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal details with third-party advertising companies. We only share information with:

  • Verified Developers/Sellers: When a buyer schedules a site visit, we share name/phone details to coordinate the physical audit.
  • Legal Clearance Partners: With your consent, we pass title documents to legal consultants conducting encumbrance checks.
  • Regulatory Authorities: Where required by land registration laws or judicial orders.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have full ownership control over your data saved on Farmland Bazaar. You may exercise these rights at any time:

  • Access & correction: Update personal details, contact coordinates, or passwords in your Account Profile feature panel.
  • Communication preferences: Toggle automatic email and phone notification settings inside the Settings panel.
  • Account erasure: Submit a request to support to permanently delete your registration logs and property lists.
